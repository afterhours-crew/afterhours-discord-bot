# :scroll: Rules

## Follow them or you shall be banned.

# 1. Be respectful
no harassment, hate speech or personal attacks. Argue with ideas, not people.

# 2. No piracy
don't share cracked copies of games, leaked builds, or links to them, and DO NOT talk about workarounds to activations, licenses, or DRM. ESPECIALLY for the games we actually work with (Need for Speed 2015). Legality is important.

# 3. No cheats for online play
we're here to preserve and mod games, not to ruin them for others.

# 4. Stay on topic
use the right channel.

# 5. No NSFW content.

# 6. No spam or self-promo without asking staff first.

# 7. Don't share personal info, yours or anyone else's.

# 8. Credit other people's work
ask before reposting someone's research, tools or mods.

# 9. Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Staff have the final say. Problem? Ping a Moderator.
